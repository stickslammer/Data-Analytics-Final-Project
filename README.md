Monterey County Housing Market Analysis (2015–2025)

Client (Hypothetical)

Monterey County Housing Market Research Initiative

Role

Junior Data Analyst

Phase 1: Ask

This is the first phase of the Google Data Analytics process.

Business Problem

Problem Statement

The Monterey County housing market experienced significant changes between 2015 and 2025. Homebuyers, sellers, real estate professionals, and local policymakers need a clear understanding of how the market evolved over this period and which economic and market factors are associated with those changes.

Primary Research Question

How has the Monterey County housing market evolved from 2015 through 2025, and which economic and market factors are most strongly associated with those changes?
