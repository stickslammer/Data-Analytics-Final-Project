# Project Charter

## Project Title

Monterey County Housing Market Analysis: 2015–2025

## Business Problem

The Monterey County housing market has experienced significant changes over the past decade. Homebuyers, sellers, real estate professionals, and local policymakers need a clear understanding of how the market has evolved and which economic and market factors are associated with those changes.

## Primary Research Question

How has the Monterey County housing market evolved from 2015 through 2025, and which economic and market factors are most strongly associated with those changes?

## Stakeholders

Primary stakeholders include:

* Prospective homebuyers
* Current homeowners
* Real estate agents and brokers
* Local government officials
* Housing planners

Secondary stakeholders include:

* Mortgage lenders
* Real estate investors
* Builders and developers
* Economic development organizations

## Business Tasks

The analysis will investigate:

* Changes in median home prices from 2015–2025
* Changes in housing inventory
* Changes in homes sold and market activity
* Differences in housing trends across Monterey County communities
* Relationships between housing prices and mortgage interest rates
* Relationships between housing prices and inflation
* Relationships between housing prices and unemployment
* Major shifts before, during, and after the COVID-19 pandemic

## Scope

The primary geographic focus is Monterey County, California.

The primary analysis period is January 2015 through December 2025.

The project will focus primarily on residential housing market trends using publicly available data.

## Data Sources Needed

Potential datasets include:

* California Association of REALTORS housing market data
* Federal Reserve or FRED mortgage-rate data
* Consumer Price Index data
* Monterey County unemployment data
* Population estimates
* Supplemental housing data from Zillow, Redfin, or FHFA for validation where appropriate

## Tools

The project may use:

* Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel
* SQL
* Python or Jupyter Notebook
* Tableau
* Git and GitHub

## Deliverables

The final project will include:

* Cleaned and documented datasets
* Data dictionary
* Data cleaning log
* Exploratory data analysis
* SQL and/or Python analysis
* Tableau dashboard
* Executive summary
* Final GitHub portfolio repository

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it:

* Clearly explains how the Monterey County housing market changed between 2015 and 2025
* Identifies important market and economic relationships without overstating causation
* Uses reproducible and well-documented data preparation steps
* Presents findings through clear and accessible visualizations
* Produces an interactive Tableau dashboard suitable for a professional portfolio
* Provides useful insights for homebuyers, sellers, real estate professionals, and policymakers

## Limitations

Potential limitations include:

* Differences in methodology across data providers
* Missing or incomplete historical observations
* Differences between county-level and city-level datasets
* Inconsistent monthly, quarterly, and annual reporting periods
* Observational data that supports association analysis but not necessarily causal conclusions
