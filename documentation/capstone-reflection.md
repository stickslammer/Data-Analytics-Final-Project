# Capstone Project Reflection

## What do I hope to take away from this capstone project?

My primary goal for this capstone project is to gain experience completing an end-to-end data analytics project using a real-world dataset. Rather than simply completing the project as a requirement for the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate, I want to create an analysis that demonstrates how data can be used to answer meaningful questions and support decision-making.

For this project, I will analyze how the Monterey County housing market changed between 2015 and 2025 and investigate economic and market factors associated with those changes.

One important skill I hope to develop is the ability to take a broad real-world question and transform it into a structured, reproducible data analysis. This includes identifying appropriate data sources, evaluating data quality, cleaning and combining datasets, performing exploratory analysis, creating visualizations, and communicating findings to stakeholders.

## Which skills do I most look forward to demonstrating?

I most look forward to demonstrating my ability to combine the individual skills I have learned throughout the Google Data Analytics program into a complete analytics workflow.

These skills include:

* Defining a business problem and developing measurable research questions
* Finding and evaluating appropriate data sources
* Cleaning and preparing real-world data
* Using spreadsheets, SQL, and other analytical tools to explore data
* Identifying trends, relationships, and anomalies
* Creating effective visualizations in Tableau
* Building an interactive dashboard
* Communicating analytical findings to both technical and non-technical audiences
* Using Git and GitHub to document the development of the project and maintain a reproducible workflow

I am particularly interested in demonstrating that I can move beyond individual exercises and use these skills together to solve an open-ended analytical problem.

## What are some issues I might encounter?

One of the biggest challenges will likely be working with data from multiple sources. Housing prices, inventory, mortgage rates, inflation, unemployment, and population data may use different time periods, geographic levels, formats, and definitions.

Some datasets may also contain missing values, inconsistent reporting periods, or changes in methodology over the ten-year period.

Another important challenge will be distinguishing correlation from causation. For example, housing prices may change at the same time as mortgage rates, unemployment, or inflation, but this does not necessarily mean that one variable directly caused the other. The analysis will therefore focus on identifying relationships and associations that are supported by the available data.

Other potential issues include:

* Finding consistent historical housing data for 2015–2025
* Reconciling monthly, quarterly, and annual datasets
* Differences between county-level and city-level data
* Handling missing or incomplete observations
* Standardizing dates and geographic identifiers
* Determining which variables are meaningful enough to include
* Avoiding misleading visualizations or unsupported conclusions
* Maintaining clear documentation as the analysis evolves

Addressing these challenges will be an important part of the project because real-world data analysis rarely begins with a perfectly clean and complete dataset.
